### Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
```
Losing houseplants to overwatering? Flowers helps you care without stress: it doesn't say "water now", it says "check the soil", and suggests what to do next.

🌱 WHAT FLOWERS DOES

1. GENTLE REMINDERS
A daily nudge to check soil and plant condition. Soil still damp? Snooze the check for 1–3 days with one tap. No penalties, no red marks.

2. KNOWLEDGE PODS
Short tips on each plant card: how to test soil moisture with a finger, where to place the pot, the most common mistake for that species.

3. PLANT GUIDE
A built-in catalog of 20 popular houseplants (ZZ plant, monstera, peace lily, snake plant, ficus and more) that works offline.

4. AI CARE CARD
Your plant isn't in the guide? Type its name and AI prepares a care card. Internet required. It is advice, not a guarantee: please verify it. For AI cards the app does not claim a plant is safe for pets or children. If an answer is wrong or unsafe, tap the report button ("Поскаржитися на відповідь ШІ" in the Ukrainian-only 1.0 interface).

5. OFFLINE CONDITION CHECKLIST
Yellow leaves or drooping stems? Go through a short check (leaves, stem, soil, pests): the app compares symptoms with your watering log and suggests what to check.

6. CARE LOG
Record waterings and condition checks for each plant.

🔒 PRIVACY
• No sign-up, no accounts.
• Your plants and log stay on your phone.
• Internet is used only for the AI card (the plant name is sent to Google; on the free tier Google may use requests to improve its products) and for reporting an AI answer.
• No ads, no analytics. Don't put personal data into the plant name.
• For users aged 18+.

The app does not replace a vet or doctor. If a pet or child ate part of a plant, contact a vet or doctor immediately.
```
